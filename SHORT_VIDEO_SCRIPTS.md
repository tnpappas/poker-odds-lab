# Poker Logic Lab — Short Video Scripts (Batch 1)

_Five ready-to-produce shorts, 25 to 40 seconds each, one per content pillar. Format for each: HOOK (first 2 seconds, say it AND put it on screen) → VOICEOVER → ON-SCREEN TEXT → VISUAL → CTA. Post the same clip to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels._

---

## 1. "You Folded a Winner" (signature / hand breakdown)

**Hook:** "You folded the best hand tonight. And you'll do it again."

**Voiceover:**
"Turn card. You've got a flush draw. Villain bets half the pot and it feels scary, so you fold. Here's the problem. A half-pot bet only needs you to win 25 percent of the time. Your flush draw wins about 36. You just folded a hand that makes money. Losing players fold here. Winners call, every time, and let the math print."

**On-screen text (timed):**
- "Half pot bet = you need 25%"
- "Flush draw = 36%"
- "That's a CALL"

**Visual:** A board and two hole cards. Numbers pop in as you say them. Green checkmark on "CALL."

**CTA:** "I built a trainer that drills this until it's automatic. Link in bio."

## 2. "The Only Math You Need" (fast tip)

**Hook:** "This is the only poker math you need at the table."

**Voiceover:**
"Count your outs. The cards that turn your hand into a winner. On the flop, multiply by four. On the turn, multiply by two. That's your chance to hit, close enough. Flush draw is nine outs. On the flop, nine times four is 36 percent. Done. No app, no calculator, three seconds in your head."

**On-screen text:**
- "Outs x 4 on the flop"
- "Outs x 2 on the turn"
- "9 outs = 36%"

**Visual:** Big numbers. A hand counting outs on a board.

**CTA:** "More table-ready math on the site. Link in bio."

## 3. "Stop Putting Him On One Hand" (range reading / differentiator)

**Hook:** "Stop asking what he has. Ask what he COULD have."

**Voiceover:**
"Amateurs put their opponent on one exact hand, and they're usually wrong. Good players put them on a range. Every hand they'd play this way. Then they ask, how do I do against all of it? He raised preflop, bet the flop, and bet again on a scary turn. That's not one hand. That's top pair and better. Your read isn't 'he has aces.' Your read is a range. That one shift is the whole game."

**On-screen text:**
- "Don't guess ONE hand"
- "Read the RANGE"

**Visual:** A 13x13 hand grid lighting up a cluster of hands instead of one cell.

**CTA:** "I made a tool that trains exactly this. Link in bio."

## 4. "You're Not Unlucky" (mindset / shareable)

**Hook:** "You're not unlucky. You just can't see your own mistakes."

**Voiceover:**
"You made a great call, lost the pot, and tilted. But losing the hand doesn't mean the call was wrong. Good poker isn't about winning this pot. It's about making the play that wins money over a thousand of them. Judge your decisions, not your results. Do that, and the bad beats stop stinging, and your real leaks finally show up."

**On-screen text:**
- "Judge the DECISION, not the result."

**Visual:** A losing hand at showdown, then a green "+EV" stamp over it.

**CTA:** "Train your decisions, not your luck. Link in bio."

## Production notes

- **Hook is everything.** Say it in the first 2 seconds and put it on screen as text. If the first line is weak, nothing else matters.
- **Captions always on.** Most people watch muted. Every word on screen.
- **Vertical 9:16.** Fill the frame.
- **One idea per video.** Don't teach two things.
- **Soft CTA only.** "Link in bio," never a hard sell. The value is the sell.
- **Batch these.** Film or generate all five in one sitting, post one per day.
