## Posting steps (same for both)

- Get the video onto your phone
  - AirDrop is not available on Windows, so email the FINAL.mp4 to yourself or drop it in Google Drive and save it to the camera roll
- Upload
  - TikTok > plus button > Upload > pick the video > Next
- Do not add a sound. The voice is the audio. If TikTok suggests a sound, skip it
- Cover
  - Select cover > scrub to a frame with the avatar looking at camera and mouth closed (around 1 to 2 seconds in) > Add text > type the cover text above > Save
- Paste the description with hashtags into the caption box
- Settings on the post screen
  - Allow comments: on
  - Allow Duet and Stitch: on
  - Save to device: off (you already have it)
- Post
- After posting
  - Reply to the first comments within the first hour; TikTok weighs early engagement
  - Do not put a URL in a comment, comment links are not clickable
